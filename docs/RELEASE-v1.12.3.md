# v1.12.3 — Reusable battle-writing methods / 可复用战斗写作经验

## 中文

公开版撤掉四篇替代教学故事及全文索引，改为独立表述的可复用写作经验；不加入原篇句子、对白、情节摘录或换名故事。本地私有版四篇原稿与原索引保持原样。

经验覆盖人物声线与选择、合理入场动机、动作空间与受力、反馈驱动变招、强弱差、未知信息、身份控制下的性格、幽默与第四墙、对白和动作节奏、铺垫回收及明确战果。按本场难点选读，不固定剧情、篇幅、回合或结局。

独立评级先行、完整九维、量级与能力交互、人设和世界观核查及现有工具不变。中英说明、调用入口和公开包清单同步更新。历史版本未重写。

## English

The public package removes the four replacement teaching stories and their full-text index. They are replaced by independently worded, reusable writing methods, without source-story sentences, dialogue, plot excerpts or renamed stories. Private local originals and their index remain unchanged.

The methods cover character voice and choices, motivated encounters, spatial/physical action, feedback-driven tactics, power differences, limited knowledge, characterization under identity control, humor and fourth-wall awareness, dialogue/action rhythm, setup/payoff and explicit outcomes. Guidance is selected by the current writing problem rather than imposed as a fixed plot.

Independent ratings, all nine attributes, ability interactions, characterization/world checks and executable tools remain unchanged. Bilingual documentation, routing and the public manifest are updated. Historical versions are not rewritten.

验证 / Validation: 67 项写作回归测试通过；Skill 格式、236 项文件清单与 Markdown 文件链接通过；本地 6 个受保护文件指纹不变。Executable scripts are unchanged; the previous full-suite baseline is reused. These checks do not certify literary quality.
